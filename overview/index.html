What the coach does for you

Four things, working together while you lift.

  • Pose detection

    Tracks your joints from any camera and draws them over your body, so you can see your own form.

  • Audio feedback

    Spoken cues tell you what to fix, so you never have to look at the screen mid-set.

  • Rep counter

    Counts every rep automatically so you can focus on the lift, not on the number.

  • Real-time response

    Feedback arrives in about 100 ms, fast enough to fix the rep you're on.
